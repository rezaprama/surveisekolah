# School visit questions

An independent guide by Reza Prama. It began with 25 topics in two photographs of notes, edited into neutral questions and expanded with 11 topics relevant to families in Indonesia.

Not a ranking, accreditation, or statistical survey. This project does not represent a government body or school. Adapt it for preschool, primary, junior secondary, or senior secondary school; skip what is not relevant.

[Use the interactive checklist](https://surveisekolah.rezaprama.com/en/#tanya)

## Learning

### Q01. How do children learn each day? (Core)

A curriculum name does not describe daily learning. Ask about teaching methods, language, play, and practical activities.

**Evidence to request:** A sample timetable, learning activities, and support for children new to the teaching language.

_From photos 1._

### Q15. What support is offered when learning is difficult? (Core)

Look for early identification, realistic goals, and regular review rather than extra worksheets alone.

**Evidence to request:** An anonymised support plan, support schedule, fees, and progress review arrangements.

_From photos 15._

### Q16. How are children ready for greater challenge supported?

Look for suitable depth and challenge rather than a larger quantity of the same work.

**Evidence to request:** Enrichment examples, projects, differentiated work, and support for social and emotional needs.

_From photos 16._

### Q22. Does the classroom support comfortable learning?

Inspect furniture, ventilation, lighting, noise, and cleanliness. Air conditioning and CCTV alone do not establish quality.

**Evidence to request:** A visit during class use, maintenance arrangements, and CCTV access and retention rules.

_From photos 22._

### Q23. Can I see a sample daily lesson plan?

Compare goals, activities, and assessment with the school’s explanation. One example cannot establish year-round consistency.

**Evidence to request:** A lesson plan used in practice and anonymised learning samples.

_From photos 23._

### Q25. What support is available when changing curricula?

A new language, syllabus, or assessment approach may require adjustment. Ask about initial assessment and support costs.

**Evidence to request:** A transition plan, curriculum mapping, language support, and review dates.

_From photos 25._

### Q28. How are different learning and support needs accommodated? (Core)

Share only necessary information about your child. Ask about adjustments, support staff, and service limits.

**Evidence to request:** Accommodation examples, access, fees, family collaboration, and a process for reviewing support.

_Added._

### Q32. How are devices, the internet, and AI used?

Ask about learning goals, age-appropriate duration, content oversight, account protection, and screen-free alternatives.

**Evidence to request:** Activity examples, device rules, internet filtering, and guidance on AI and children’s data.

_Added._

## Safety

### Q03. What happens when a child reports bullying? (Core)

Children need a trusted person to approach. Ask about protection, follow-up, and communication with families.

**Evidence to request:** A written reporting process, named role, response timeline, and an anonymised case example.

_From photos 3._

### Q05. Who supervises children outside the classroom? (Core)

Inspect playgrounds, stairs, fences, and entrances. Ask about break-time supervision as well as security staff.

**Evidence to request:** Supervision rosters, visitor controls, playground inspections, slip-resistant surfaces, and barriers.

_From photos 5._

### Q10. Who responds when a child is injured? (Core)

Ask about relevant first aid and basic life support training, validity, and backup staff.

**Evidence to request:** Current training records, first aid supplies, emergency contacts, and a healthcare referral process.

_From photos 10._

### Q21. How are school trips organised?

Ask about opting out, supervision, transport, medication, and the response if a child becomes separated.

**Evidence to request:** Consent forms, a risk plan, supervision details, emergency contacts, and itemised costs.

_From photos 21._

### Q24. How is a person collecting a child verified? (Core)

Children should be released only to authorised people. Changes need confirmation through an official channel.

**Evidence to request:** An authorised collection list, identity checks, change procedures, and late collection arrangements.

_From photos 24._

### Q29. What happens during an earthquake, flood, or fire? (Core)

Match questions to local risks. Families need assembly points, updates, and emergency collection procedures.

**Evidence to request:** Evacuation routes, recent drills, assembly points, disability support, and family reunification procedures.

_Added._

### Q30. How are allergies and health conditions managed? (Core)

Ask about essential health information, exposure prevention, medication storage, consent, and emergency response.

**Evidence to request:** An individual plan where needed, responsible staff, medication rules, and incident notification.

_Added._

## Teachers

### Q02. How many children and teachers share a class? (Core)

Distinguish lead teachers, assistants, and maximum class size. Ask who covers absences.

**Evidence to request:** Actual enrolment, class limits, assistant roles, and substitute arrangements.

_From photos 2._

### Q08. How does the school support teacher wellbeing?

Workload and staff turnover affect continuity for children. Do not request private salary details.

**Evidence to request:** Workload policies, planning time, training, staff support, and an overview of staff turnover.

_From photos 8._

### Q11. What psychological support is available?

Distinguish counsellors, support teachers, and psychologists. Ask about access, confidentiality, consent, and extra fees.

**Evidence to request:** Provider qualifications, service hours, referral procedures, and fee information.

_From photos 11._

### Q33. How do teachers respond to challenging behaviour?

Ask for practical examples of conflict or distress. Look for responses that preserve dignity and avoid humiliation.

**Evidence to request:** A behaviour policy, response examples, teacher training, and family involvement.

_Added._

### Q35. How do teachers continue developing their practice?

Ask about relevant qualifications, lesson observation, feedback, and training applied in class.

**Evidence to request:** An overview of qualifications, development plans, and examples of practice changed after training.

_Added._

## Daily life

### Q04. How does the school manage the MBG programme?

Makan Bergizi Gratis, Indonesia’s free nutritious meals programme, is not a standalone quality measure. Check food safety or alternative meal arrangements.

**Evidence to request:** The provider or SPPG kitchen, responsible staff, menu, complaints process, and dietary alternatives.

_From photos 4._

### Q06. How are children helped with toileting?

For younger children, assistance should support safety, independence, and bodily privacy.

**Evidence to request:** Child-accessible toilets, water and soap, assistance procedures, and accident handling.

_From photos 6._

### Q07. How do toilet arrangements protect children’s privacy?

Check age-appropriate arrangements, doors, safe locks, and access to help.

**Evidence to request:** Inspect facilities and ask about separation, accessibility, and supervision that preserves privacy.

_From photos 7._

### Q13. How are birthday celebrations handled?

Ask about opting out, spending limits, food, allergies, and preventing exclusion.

**Evidence to request:** Celebration rules, invitation arrangements, food choices, and no-cost alternatives.

_From photos 13._

### Q18. What are meal times and breaks like?

Check time for eating, drinking, movement, and rest, without forcing children to finish food.

**Evidence to request:** A daily schedule, meal supervision, drinking water access, and packed-lunch rules.

_From photos 18._

### Q19. How are canteens and nearby food vendors managed?

Ask about hygiene, children leaving school grounds, and responsibility for food complaints.

**Evidence to request:** Canteen rules, hygiene checks, gate controls, and a complaints channel.

_From photos 19._

### Q20. What are the snack and drink rules?

Ask about sugar, salt, sweetened drinks, and food shared at events. Compare rules with daily practice.

**Evidence to request:** Sample menus or canteen stock lists and food rules for class events.

_From photos 20._

## Family

### Q09. How will I hear about my child’s progress? (Core)

Agree on channels, frequency, and response times. Distinguish learning updates from incident notifications.

**Evidence to request:** An anonymised report, meeting schedule, official channels, and urgent communication procedures.

_From photos 9._

### Q12. What are the rules on gifts for teachers?

Clear rules help families avoid pressure to give gifts or seek preferential treatment.

**Evidence to request:** Written policies on gifts, class collections, and conflicts of interest.

_From photos 12._

### Q14. How often are parents expected to attend?

Families need advance notice, including working parents and carers with limited availability.

**Evidence to request:** An events calendar, required attendance, expected costs, and remote participation options.

_From photos 14._

### Q17. How are rules applied fairly to every family?

Ask about discipline, service access, and disputes regardless of a parent’s status or public profile.

**Evidence to request:** Equal-treatment policies, a complaints route, and an anonymised dispute example.

_From photos 17._

### Q31. Who may use my child’s photos and information?

Ask about publication, messaging groups, apps, CCTV, and declining publicity without losing learning access.

**Evidence to request:** Separate consent choices, purposes, access, retention periods, and withdrawal procedures.

_Added._

## Fees & access

### Q26. What is the full cost of one school year? (Core)

Include admission, tuition, uniforms, books, meals, transport, trips, and support. Ask about increases and refunds.

**Evidence to request:** An itemised fee sheet, mandatory and optional costs, payment dates, withdrawal terms, and financial aid.

_Added._

### Q27. What is the school’s official identity and status?

Match its name and address with official records. Accreditation is one input and does not replace a visit.

**Evidence to request:** The NPSN school identifier, relevant operating permit, and accreditation status through official services.

_Added._

### Q34. What are the admission and trial arrangements?

Understand assessments, age readiness, documents, deadlines, and financial commitments before paying.

**Evidence to request:** Written requirements, observation procedures, trial opportunities, and cancellation terms.

_Added._

### Q36. Can my child access the journey and facilities?

Try the route at school time. Check crossings, transport, and accessibility for children or carers with disabilities.

**Evidence to request:** Actual journey time, transport procedures, entrances, ramps where needed, and usable toilets.

_Added._
